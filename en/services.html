FAQ

Before you write in, this might already answer it

Why hire a freelancer instead of a big consultancy?

No sales layers or middle management: you talk to me from the first email through report delivery, which means a tighter price without losing technical rigor.

How is confidentiality of my data guaranteed?

We sign an NDA before any work begins. All findings and evidence are handled under GDPR and deleted at project close if you request it.

What if you don't find any vulnerabilities?

You still receive a report with the methodology applied and scope covered — useful as evidence of due diligence for clients, insurers or compliance audits.

When and how do I pay?

Typically 50% when confirming the engagement and 50% on report delivery. For smaller projects, a single payment on delivery. No surprises: the price is fixed before starting.

Do you only work in Cádiz, or across Spain?

100% remote across Spain. The initial consultation and results presentation are done by video call, no travel required.

Do you also fix the vulnerabilities you find?

The service covers detecting, documenting and prioritizing. Your technical team implements the fix, but I can support the process and validate that the fix closes the issue.

Individuals

Frequently asked questions for individuals

What should I do if I think a personal account has been hacked?

Message me as soon as possible, ideally within the first 24-48 hours — the sooner we act, the easier it is to shut down access and limit the damage. In a video call we'll go through what happened together.

Is it confidential if my case is personal, not a business?

Yes, exactly the same as with businesses: I can sign an NDA if you need one, and your data is handled under GDPR, deleted once the case closes if you ask.

Do I need to bring in my device, or can everything be done remotely?

Most cases are resolved remotely via screen share. Only expert-witness cases requiring deep forensic extraction might need the physical device.

Can I use your report to file a police report?

Yes, the legally valid report is designed exactly for that: documenting evidence with expert-witness methodology to support a police report or legal proceedings.

What happens if my account can't ultimately be recovered?

With the account recovery service, you only pay the handling fee (€40-€60) if it isn't recovered — never the full amount.

Do you handle harassment or cyberstalking cases, not just stolen accounts?

Yes. It's one of the most common reasons for the legally valid report: documenting messages, screenshots and harassment patterns so they hold up as evidence.
